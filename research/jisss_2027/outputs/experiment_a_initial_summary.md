# Experiment A - Initial Frozen-Specification Validation

## Status

Model parameters remain frozen.

No prospective observation has been used for calibration or model fitting.

## Dataset status

Retrospective observations: 12
Prospective observations: 6
Prospective observations eligible for validation review: 6

## Initial post-freeze findings

### Hormuz persistence

Reuters-derived observations show 5 commodity-vessel transits on
25 August 2026 compared with a 10-day average of 15.

This represents an observed shortfall of approximately 66.7%.

A separate S&P Global observation published 28 August reported
Hormuz crossings remained more than 80% below pre-conflict conditions.

These observations provide post-freeze evidence that severe physical
impairment persisted after the 19 August model freeze.

### Alternative-corridor behavior

Bab el-Mandeb recorded 31 commodity-vessel transits on 25 August and
was described as roughly consistent with its recent operating level.

This observation is relevant to the model's structural treatment of
alternative regional routing but is not by itself evidence of causal
rerouting from Hormuz.

### Price observations

Brent settled at $87.84/bbl on 26 August.

Dubai crude was assessed at $90.40/bbl on 27 August.

These observations will be retained for market-response analysis but
will not initially be treated as point-error tests against modeled peak
price proxies because the observed and modeled statistics differ in
timing and construction.

## Initial conclusion

The first post-freeze evidence supports continued severe impairment of
Hormuz transit while showing comparatively greater functionality in the
Bab el-Mandeb corridor.

This is preliminary frozen-specification validation evidence, not
retrospective calibration and not proof of predictive accuracy.

## Next requirement

Expand the post-freeze observation set before calculating aggregate
validation scores such as directional accuracy or scenario-band
classification accuracy.
