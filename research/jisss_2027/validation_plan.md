# Validation Plan

## Primary validation domains

1. Hormuz physical throughput
2. Bab el-Mandeb / Red Sea throughput
3. Crude price response
4. Regional sour-crude basis response
5. LNG disruption
6. Refined-product disruption
7. Alternative pipeline utilization
8. Inventory drawdowns
9. Production shut-ins
10. Freight and war-risk cost
11. Immediate versus lagged transmission

## Comparison rules

Only observations with independently documented values and dates may
enter quantitative validation.

Contextual reporting may support interpretation but must not be converted
into synthetic observed values.

Practitioner-derived evidence remains non-driving unless independently
verified through public evidence.

## Initial metrics

- absolute error
- percentage error where meaningful
- MAE
- RMSE
- directional accuracy
- interval coverage
- scenario classification accuracy
- rank-order agreement

## Freeze rule

No model parameter may be altered until Experiment A results and metrics
have been written to the outputs directory.
